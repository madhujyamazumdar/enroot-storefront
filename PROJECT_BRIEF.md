# Enroot Store Project Brief

## Brand and audience
- Store name: Enroot
- Products: Clothing and fashion
- Target audience: Young adults
- Brand personality: Minimal, timeless, premium, natural, classy, and royal

## Launch collection
- Launch with 1-10 products
- Price range: Under INR 1,500
- Use elegant image placeholders until product photography is available

## Website
- Pages: Home, Shop, Product Details, Cart, Checkout, About, and Contact
- Theme: Minimal and aesthetic, balanced between forest-green sections and crisp white sections with subtle muted-gold accents
- Typography: Elegant serif inspired by luxury fragrance branding
- Visual reference: The Enroot brand image shared during discovery

## Payments and contact
- Payment options: UPI and Cash on Delivery
- Customer contact: WhatsApp, phone, and email
- Checkout handoff: Send order details through WhatsApp
- UPI ID, QR code, WhatsApp number, phone, and email must be replaced with the owner's real details before launch

## Features
- Offers banner, customer reviews, size guide, shipping information, FAQ, and WhatsApp chat button
