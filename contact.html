The useful details

A few things you might wonder.

How much is shipping?

Complimentary shipping is offered on orders over ₹999. Shipping charges and estimated delivery timing for other orders will be confirmed before dispatch.

When will my order arrive?

Delivery timing depends on your location. We'll confirm an estimated delivery date with you on WhatsApp when your order is placed.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Please contact us within 7 days of delivery if you need help with a return or exchange. Items should be unused and in their original condition. Confirm your final policy before launch.

Which payment methods can I use?

UPI and Cash on Delivery are available. For UPI orders, we'll share verified payment details after confirming your order on WhatsApp.
