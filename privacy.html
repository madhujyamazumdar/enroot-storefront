Privacy notice.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Information recorded with an order

When you submit an order, Enroot records your name, WhatsApp phone number, optional email address, delivery address and instructions, the items and quantities ordered, subtotal, payment preference, order ID, order time, and the time and version of the privacy notice you accepted.

We do not ask for or store card numbers, bank passwords, or UPI PINs.

How we use it

We use these details to review and fulfill your order, contact you about delivery or payment, provide customer support, and maintain business records. Checkout opens WhatsApp chats so you can send the order to Enroot's listed contact numbers. The order is recorded even if you do not send those WhatsApp messages.

Storage and access

Order records are stored in Enroot's private Netlify Blobs store. Netlify states that Blobs are encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to the order dashboard is restricted by a private admin token. Keep that token and access to the Enroot Netlify project private.

Order data is stored in Netlify's Singapore region. We do not sell customer order information. We only share details when needed to process, deliver, or support an order, or when required by law.

Retention and requests

We keep order records only as long as reasonably needed for fulfillment, support, and applicable business or legal requirements. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of your order information, contact enrootofficiall@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.
